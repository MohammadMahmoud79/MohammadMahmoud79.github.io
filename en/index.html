Professional experience

Broadcast mentoring and editorial quality

I served as a senior/experienced broadcaster for general external broadcasts, including presidential, artistic and religious coverage, as well as news bulletins and extended live and cultural programmes. My responsibilities included training and technically guiding newly assigned broadcaster cohorts, reviewing their technical and editorial performance, following up on programme execution under management direction, and submitting assessments, analysis and programme recommendations—including suggestions for presenters, guests and supporting recorded materials. These responsibilities covered successive cohorts, including those in 2006, 2009, 2010 and subsequent years.

I also served as Team Leader in work associated with the contribution to the book Israel and the Middle East.
